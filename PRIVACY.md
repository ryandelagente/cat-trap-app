# Privacy Policy — Cat Trap Adventure

**Last updated:** 26 July 2026

This privacy policy describes how the **Cat Trap Adventure** mobile
application ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it.

## The short version

- The App itself does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal
  information about you.
- The App does **not** require an account, sign-in, or any personal details.
- The App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may
  collect device identifiers and other data as described below. This is the
  only third-party data collection in the App.
- The App stores your game progress (current level) locally on your device
  only. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Information collected by the App itself

**None.** The App does not include analytics, crash reporting, or any other
telemetry SDK. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone,
location, or files.

## Information collected by Google AdMob

The App shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements via
Google's AdMob SDK. When an ad is served, Google may collect information
including:

- Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completions)

Google uses this information to serve, measure, and personalise ads, and to
prevent fraud. Google's use of this data is governed by:

- **Google Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:** https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **AdMob & AdSense data collection notice:** https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can reset or opt out of personalised advertising at any time from your
Android device settings:
**Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID** (or reset it).

## Rewarded ads

If you tap the "Watch an ad to skip this level" button, an AdMob rewarded
video ad plays. You may close the ad at any time; you only receive the
in-game reward (level skip) if the ad plays to completion. No additional
data beyond what AdMob normally collects is transmitted.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under
13 has used the App, no personal information collected by us needs to be
deleted because we collect none. For AdMob's handling of ads served to
users under 13, see Google's Families Policy:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335

## Data retention

Because the App does not collect any personal information, there is nothing
for us to retain or delete. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained
according to Google's own retention policies (see links above).

## Your rights

Since we do not collect personal information about you, we have nothing to
provide, correct, or delete on request. For any data collected by Google
AdMob, please exercise your rights directly with Google via the links above.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change how information is handled, this policy
will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material
changes will also be announced in the app's Play Store listing.
